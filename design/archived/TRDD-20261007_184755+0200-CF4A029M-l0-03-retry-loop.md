## Steps

1. Read `computeBackoffMs` and its callers; call it, never copy it.
2. `parseRetryAfter(headers, now)`: `retry-after-ms` integer wins; else `Retry-After` as integer seconds or HTTP-date; garbage or past gives 0.
3. `isRetryable(status, errorBody)` as one table: 408, 409, 429, 500–599, and 402 only with `limit_source: openrouter_in_flight_budget`.
4. Loop with attempts clamped to 1..6 (default 3): drain the body before waiting; wait = max(backoff, retry-after); if the wait passes the deadline, throw `rate-limited` with the wait in the error.
5. Network errors and timeouts throw at once with "the request may have been billed" (D7).
6. WHY comments on steps 2, 4 and 5.
The wait between attempts takes L0-04's deadline/abort signal; Ctrl-C during a wait (E10) is owned and tested by L0-04 (plan §12).
